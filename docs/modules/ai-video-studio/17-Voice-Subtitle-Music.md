# EIGU Platform — AI Video Module

AI Video Module — Voice, Subtitle & Music

## Voice

- Voice library: lưu trong `userData/voices/`.
- Generated voice: lưu trong `.eigu` → `assets/audio/` (embedded).
- Language, accent, emotion, speed, pitch.

## Subtitle

- ASR/transcription.
- Manual edit.
- Timing, animation, highlight.
- Multi-language tracks.
- Lưu trong `project.json` → subtitles[].

## Music

- AI-selected music.
- Uploaded music.
- Volume, loop, fade, ducking.
- Music file lưu trong `.eigu` → `assets/audio/` hoặc external reference.

## Licensing

Asset audio/music phải có metadata về nguồn và quyền sử dụng.
